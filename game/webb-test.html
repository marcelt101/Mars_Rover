JAMES WEBB SPACE TELESCOPE

Webb floats 1.5 million kilometers from Earth, always keeping its sunshield between the Sun and its gold mirror. The sky behind it is the real sky, made from 1.7 billion real stars.

Find the Cosmic Cliffs

It is June 2022. Steer Webb to the Carina Nebula, deep in the southern sky. Follow the arrow, then zoom in.

The Cosmic Cliffs

This is the edge of a giant bubble in the Carina Nebula, 7,600 light-years away. Hot young stars blew it open with their light and winds. Webb's infrared camera sees through the dust and reveals hundreds of baby stars that were hidden before.
