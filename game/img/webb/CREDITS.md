# Webb mission pictures

- `webb.webp`, `webb-small.webp`: the James Webb Space Telescope, cut out of NASA's artist's conception of Webb
  fully deployed (2021). Credit: NASA GSFC/CIL/Adriana Manrique Gutierrez. Public domain.
- `sky8k.jpg`, `sky4k.jpg`: the whole sky from NASA's Deep Star Maps 2020 (SVS 4851), tone-mapped from the 8K
  EXR. Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio; Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC.
  Made from 1.7 billion stars in the Hipparcos-2, Tycho-2 and Gaia DR2 catalogs. Equirectangular, celestial
  coordinates, 0h right ascension in the middle, right ascension increasing to the left.
- `t_carina.jpg`: the Cosmic Cliffs in NGC 3324, Carina Nebula. Webb NIRCam, July 2022.
  Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI.
- `webb-corner.webp`: the small telescope in the corner of the telescope view. Supplied by the game's author; it is
  NASA's artist's conception of Webb (credit NASA). The checkerboard painted into the downloaded file was removed.
