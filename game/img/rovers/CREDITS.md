# Rover pictures (top-down)

Made by the game's author for Crater Crew. Not NASA images.

- `spirit-top-512.webp`, `spirit-top-256.webp`: Spirit, a Mars Exploration Rover, seen from above (the Scout).
  White background removed for the game.
- `opportunity-top-512.webp`, `opportunity-top-256.webp`: Opportunity, Spirit's twin, seen from above (the Hauler).
  Supplied with a transparent background; centered on a square for the game.
- `sojourner-top-512.webp`, `sojourner-top-256.webp`: Sojourner, the first Mars rover (1997), seen from above (the Cadets' Buggy).
  Supplied with a transparent background; centered on a square for the game.
- `perseverance-top-*.webp` (Science and Excavator rovers), `ingenuity-top-*.webp` (the helicopter), `zhurong-top-*.webp`
  (Utopia Planitia): from the author's sprite pack (AI-made game artwork, "not verified engineering-accurate replicas").
  Supplied with transparent backgrounds; centered on squares for the game.
- `lab-top-*.webp` (the Lab) and `pod-top-*.webp` (the base station on Mars): the author's building pictures, drawn at a slight
  angle and never rotated. Supplied with transparent backgrounds; centered on squares for the game.
