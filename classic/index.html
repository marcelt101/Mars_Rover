    How it works
    1. Tap commands to build today's sequence. You get up to 10 per sol (a Mars day).
    2. Press Send to Mars. Real signals take 4 to 24 minutes to arrive. Here it takes a few seconds.
    3. Watch the rover. If it sees a hazard, it stops itself and skips the rest of the plan.
    4. Every night the solar panels add 25% battery. Finish in fewer sols to earn more stars.
